# ADR-0002 — Atlas AI Solution-Layer Category Lock — 13:02, 04.06.2026

## Status
Accepted.

## Decision
Public website copy presents Atlas AI as a governed AI solution layer, not as a custom-GPT storefront, generic AI consultancy, or proprietary foundation-model company.

## Rules
- Use package names such as Atlas AI Starter, Professional, Business Workspace, Research / Multi-Engine, and Managed Secure.
- Vendor names belong in Platform Lanes, provider disclosure, and fit explanations.
- Do not use vendor-branded product naming that implies official partnership unless contractually true.
