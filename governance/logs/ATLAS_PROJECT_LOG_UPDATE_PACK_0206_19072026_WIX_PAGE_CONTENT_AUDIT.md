## Issue or mistake observed

The Wix content inventory contains substantial page and CMS material, but multiple June records conflict with the later July Atlas baseline. The existing records also blur four different states: content drafted, CMS seeded, Wix Studio bound and public-release ready.

Specific contradictions and defects found:

- Current Wix site status is Draft, while Wix CMS platform and phase records say the site is published.
- Home and Managed Secure source copy contains unsupported claims involving isolation, encryption, compliance, SLA, secure channels, audit, integrations and operational availability.
- The Packages page is still based on seven legacy assistant/specialist cards while the current model is outcome-led and contains separate delivery tiers and controlled audience routes.
- The Process page stops at Phase 5 and includes stale repository, publication and ChatGPT-first statements.
- The nine old validation-gate cards are not the complete current G00–G22 release system.
- The claims registry has 22 records but only 17 unique topics because five records are duplicated.
- The FAQ, package and locale content is English-first; current Bokmål records are still marked for review.
- Contact content promises deletion, confidentiality, secure continuation and 1–2 day response without validated legal or operational evidence.
- The Wix connector did not expose a rendered page tree or usable preview URL, so final Wix visual/responsive validation could not be performed.

## Root cause

CMS seeding and draft creation occurred before the July consolidation and before the final Figma/Gamma validation cycle. Later strategy, package, route, claims and safety decisions were not systematically reconciled back into all Wix records. Earlier reports also treated source design quality as a proxy for implementation completion.

## Corrective rule

1. Treat Draft, CMS seeded, Studio bound, visually validated and release-approved as separate statuses.
2. Apply current user instruction and the July consolidated baseline before older Wix/Gamma records.
3. Use outcome-led package architecture and capability classes, not legacy assistant names or permanent provider promises.
4. Preserve business/professional as the primary route and student/consumer as controlled secondary routes.
5. Keep Managed Secure architecture-review only until evidence and public availability are explicitly approved.
6. Do not publish prices, entitlement, SLA, response-time, privacy, security or provider claims while PENDING_REVIEW.
7. Use public forms only for low-risk qualification; enforce sensitive-category stop and no-upload rules technically.
8. Deduplicate claims, refresh review dates and map old gates to G00–G22.
9. Require rendered Wix screenshots and responsive/form/accessibility tests before claiming visual or deployment readiness.
