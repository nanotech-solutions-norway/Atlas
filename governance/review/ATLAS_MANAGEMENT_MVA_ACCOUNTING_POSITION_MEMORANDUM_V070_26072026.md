## Billing, tax point and revenue recognition

- Monthly subscriptions are billed in advance for a defined monthly service period.
- Customer-facing prices are displayed including MVA.
- Invoices/receipts identify NanoTech Solutions Norway AS and include the organisation number followed by MVA.
- Revenue is recognised over the applicable monthly service period.
- Amounts relating to a later accounting period are deferred where required by the accounting cut-off.
- Output MVA is posted using the current Conta configuration and reconciled to order, payment and invoice records.
- Order records retain offer version, price, tax rate, service period and customer-jurisdiction evidence.

## Refunds and corrections

- A full withdrawal refund within the initial 14-day policy reverses gross charge, net revenue and output MVA.
- The initial release does not use proportional early-start deductions.
- Refunds and price corrections generate a credit note or equivalent correction linked to the original order.
- Duplicate charges are fully reversed unless a separate valid supply is evidenced.
- Chargebacks remain open reconciliation exceptions until payment, entitlement, invoice and accounting records agree.
- Refunds normally use the original payment method.

## Foreign provider and imported-service costs

- Foreign AI API, cloud, hosting, SaaS, development, legal, security and consultancy purchases require vendor-by-vendor review.
- Where Norwegian reverse-charge rules apply, applicable output and deductible input MVA are posted using the current Conta configuration.
- Supplier wording alone is not sufficient evidence.
- Supplier country, service description, contract, invoice, payment and posting evidence are linked.
- Provider costs are Atlas operating costs unless a separately approved recharge or agency arrangement exists.
