## Authority and scope

NanoTech Solutions Norway AS owns and operates Atlas AI. Atlas AI is an internal department/business line and not a separate legal entity.

The restricted scope is:

- Norway-only self-service B2C;
- monthly Student Basic, Student Plus and Everyday Basic;
- Student minimum age 16;
- users below 16 excluded;
- Family, subordinate accounts, annual/semester commitments, top-ups, boosts, free trials, automatic overage and foreign B2C excluded;
- hard caps, no negative balance and no automatic excess-use payment.

## Ages 16–17

For the initial release:

- the minor may be the named Student user;
- an adult payer is the contracting and payment-responsible party;
- the adult payer accepts terms, payment obligation, privacy information and any early-start request;
- the system records age band, adult payer and relationship;
- verification is proportionate and data-minimised;
- ordinary public identity-document upload is prohibited;
- checkout fails closed if age/payer controls are incomplete or inconsistent;
- the account transitions or is re-confirmed at age 18.

## Checkout and pre-contract information

Before ordering, the customer receives the seller identity, selected plan, principal functionality and limitations, VAT-inclusive price, billing interval, renewal, hard cap, cancellation route, withdrawal policy, activation timing, provider information, terms, privacy notice and complaint route.

The final order control uses an unambiguous payment-obligation formulation. The selected plan, monthly price and billing interval are visible immediately beside the control.

## Activation, withdrawal and refunds

- Default activation follows the 14-calendar-day withdrawal period.
- Immediate activation requires a separate unselected request by the adult contracting party.
- For the initial release, Atlas grants a full refund for a valid withdrawal within 14 calendar days, including where immediate access was requested or limited use occurred.
- No proportional early-start deduction is used in the initial release.
- Refunds use the original payment method unless another lawful method is documented.
- A credit note/correction record links to the original order and payment.
- Failure to generate durable confirmation blocks activation.

## Cancellation, complaints and data

- Monthly subscriptions may be cancelled at any time without a cancellation fee.
- Cancellation normally takes effect at the end of the current paid period.
- Members Area provides cancellation, with a fallback contact route.
- Cancellation and withdrawal receive durable confirmation.
- Complaints and remedies are available in Bokmål and English.
- Retention after cancellation is limited to legal, accounting, security and dispute requirements.
- Material provider, model, functionality or price changes require appropriate prior notice.

5. hard-cap and no-negative-balance controls;
6. Bokmål/English, accessibility, responsive, payment, cancellation and refund testing.
