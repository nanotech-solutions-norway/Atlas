## 1. Why the request is necessary

A separate request memorandum is not a statutory formality by itself. It is required by the Atlas release-governance model because the unresolved questions are specific to the exact Atlas offers, customer groups, checkout wording and technical implementation.

Professional review is required because:

- the payment-obligation button and immediately visible information must comply with the exact online order design;
- failure of the payment-obligation rule may mean that the consumer is not bound by the order;
- contract formation, the withdrawal-period start and durable-medium confirmation must be assigned to exact system events;
- early commencement and any proportional payment require approved wording, affirmative evidence and a lawful calculation;
- cancellation, withdrawal, refunds, credit notes, entitlement changes and accounting corrections must be aligned;
- the product minimum age is 16, while users aged 16–17 are minors and require a specifically approved contracting, payer/guardian, privacy and remedy model;
- a general research report or management signature cannot substitute for version-specific Norwegian legal advice.

An equivalent signed, dated and version-specific legal opinion may satisfy this evidence requirement. Public consumer checkout cannot receive `GO` under the current Atlas controls without accepted legal approval for the exact release candidate.
