## 2. Approved domestic MVA position

The standard 25% Norwegian MVA rate is adopted for the restricted Norway-only monthly consumer subscription scope. The position assumes an automated electronic/AI service with limited or no human intervention; the customer segment or a Student label does not create an education exemption. A material change toward human-led teaching, coaching, certification, publications or mixed supplies requires renewed classification before sale.

## 5. Refunds, withdrawals and corrections

- The initial release uses a no-deduction policy: a valid withdrawal within the statutory 14-day period triggers a full refund and full credit note reversing gross charge, net revenue/deferred revenue and output MVA.
- Atlas does not rely on proportionate payment for early performance or immediate loss of withdrawal rights for accounting/MVA implementation.
- The separate legal-review draft describes a possible proportional-payment model; it is not adopted by this memorandum. Adoption requires a versioned legal decision and amendment to refund, credit-note, revenue and MVA logic.
- Checkout must provide pre-contract withdrawal information and the withdrawal form.
- Monthly subscriptions must be easy to cancel; cancellation normally takes effect from the next payment period. The six-month recurring-agreement reminder must be implemented.
- Refunds or corrections require a compliant credit note linked to the original sales document and order.
- Duplicate charges are fully reversed unless a separate valid supply is evidenced.
- Chargebacks remain open exceptions until payment, entitlement, sales-document and accounting states agree.
- Refunds normally use the original payment method.

## 6. Foreign provider and imported-service costs

- Foreign AI API, cloud, hosting, SaaS, development, legal, security and consultancy purchases require vendor-by-vendor review.
- Where reverse-charge rules apply, NanoTech Solutions Norway AS calculates output MVA; deductible input MVA is recognised only to the extent used in MVA-taxable activity.
- Foreign VAT is not Norwegian deductible input MVA and must be separately resolved.
- Provider invoices, supplier country, service description, contract, delivery/tax point, documented NOK exchange rate, payment and posting evidence must be linked.
- Current Conta guidance supports MVA code 86 where deduction entitlement applies and code 87 where it does not, subject to live-tenant verification.
- Provisional cost accounts include 6554 for software purchased from abroad and 6780 for other services purchased from abroad. Conta system postings may use 2704 for output MVA on imported services and 2714 for deductible input MVA; system-generated vouchers must be verified rather than manually forced.
- Provider costs are Atlas operating costs unless an approved recharge or agency arrangement exists.
