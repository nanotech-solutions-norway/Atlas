## Issue or mistake observed

The Atlas project contained extensive website, business, Workspace, security, pricing and launch-gate work, but full deployment could still be interpreted too narrowly as completion of the public website plus an end-user Workspace. Customer organisation administration, Atlas operator controls, knowledge governance, workflow lifecycle, connector controls, support/status, observability and developer integration were not consolidated as mandatory operating surfaces.

## Root cause

Prior planning was organized by document/workstream category rather than by complete product operating surfaces and end-to-end state transitions. Research recommendations, architecture choices, implementation evidence and release approval also required clearer separation.

## Corrective rule

Assess full deployment across seven operating surfaces and require objective implementation and acceptance evidence for every surface included in a release profile. Treat the Control Center, authoritative entitlement/metering, support, observability, incident/recovery and reconciliation functions as product requirements rather than secondary operational tasks.
