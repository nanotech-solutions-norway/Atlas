## Current-source update

- The ordinary Norwegian 25% rate remains supportable only as a domestic working baseline for the currently described taxable digital, software, hosting, IT and consultancy supplies.
- Foreign software, API, cloud, hosting and professional-service purchases require vendor-by-vendor reverse-charge assessment and accounting configuration.
- A Norwegian supplier without an EU establishment may use the non-Union OSS route for services supplied to EU consumers, subject to approved registration, customer-location, rate, filing and record-retention controls.
- The European Commission published revised OSS explanatory material on 24.07.2026, including changes relevant from 01.01.2027. Atlas must version its OSS rules and revalidate them before launch and regulatory change.
- Wix Pricing Plans tax application is site-configured/manual and is not controlled through Pricing Plans Orders API fields. CMS metadata therefore cannot be treated as charged-tax evidence.
