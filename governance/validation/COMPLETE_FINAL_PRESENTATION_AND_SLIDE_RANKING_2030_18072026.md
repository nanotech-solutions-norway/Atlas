## Methodology

Scores use a 0–10 scale. Overall score:

- Claim content and canonical alignment: **45%**
- Visual quality: **20%**
- Readability and scanability: **15%**
- Sales focus and persuasiveness: **20%**

Sales strength cannot compensate for unsupported claims. A legal, trust or governance slide can therefore score very highly on claims while scoring lower on direct sales focus without being defective for its intended purpose.

## Presentation-level assessment

### 1. About v3

Best complete brand and sales narrative. It moves coherently from the customer problem to the Atlas method, offers, differentiation, principles and a final CTA. Claim discipline is strong and the closing slide is the second-best slide in the entire nine-deck set. The main visual weakness is unresolved imagery on Slides 1, 4 and 5.

### 2. Platform lanes v3

Best B2B solution-selection and consultative authority deck. It has the strongest individual slide overall and explains Atlas as a governed routing and implementation layer rather than a permanent-model promise.

### 3. Onboarding v3

Best operational conversion journey. The sequence from audience identification to data classification, next-step routing and architecture review is persuasive and practical. The deck would become materially stronger with final hero imagery and live intake/process evidence.

### 4. Trust v3

Highest claim-discipline deck after Legal. It addresses provider, data, responsibility, early access, Managed Secure and human control with unusually precise boundaries. Sales focus is appropriately lower because the deck is primarily an assurance and qualification asset.

### 5. Student v3

Strongest dedicated student narrative. Slides 2 and 4 explain the learning journey and use cases effectively. Proposed prices, provider functionality, age rules and activation mechanics reduce the claim score of the pricing/comparison slides.

### 6. Resources v3

Strong expertise and trust asset. Its commercial score remains constrained because most resources are planned rather than verified as live.

### 7. Consumer v3

Strong everyday-benefit storytelling and high accessibility. Family, provider and entitlement dependencies continue to reduce claim readiness.

### 8. Legal v3

Highest claim score in the complete set. Its lower total reflects its deliberately low sales focus and the dense legal-register slide, not weak legal caution. It is a navigation/governance asset, not a primary sales deck.

### 9. Prices v3

Strong objection handling and closing conversion, but the largest concentration of approval-gated commercial claims. It remains the deck most dependent on price, MVA, provider, usage, Family and entitlement approval.

## Cross-deck findings

### Claim quality

- **Legal v3** has the highest claim score: 9.94.
- **Trust v3** follows at 9.86.
- **Onboarding v3** combines very high claim discipline with stronger conversion value than Trust or Legal.
- **Prices v3** remains the lowest on claims because it contains the highest concentration of proposed commercial mechanics.

### Visual quality

- Strongest recurring structures: diagnostic/process cards, side-line cards and two-column blue/white comparisons.
- Main weakness: editable image and icon placeholders in About, Trust, Student, Onboarding, Platform, Consumer, Prices and Resources.
- Most visually dense slides: Legal slide 8, Prices slide 8, Student slide 7 and Resources slide 7.

### Readability

- The revised 150% box line spacing and content-fit sizing remain effective.
- The legal register is readable at full size but weak for presentation-distance reading.
- Tables and long validation notices are the principal remaining sources of cognitive load.

### Sales focus

- Best brand/sales deck: About v3.
- Best B2B consultative deck: Platform lanes v3.
- Best operational conversion deck: Onboarding v3.
- Best direct conversion slide: Prices v3 slide 9, with sales score 9.50.
- Best student benefit slide: Student v3 slide 4, with sales score 9.40.
- Legal and Trust intentionally prioritize qualification and trust over direct conversion.

## Recommended priority order

1. Use **About v3** as the principal brand and positioning presentation.
2. Use **Platform lanes v3** for B2B solution selection and platform-routing conversations.
3. Use **Onboarding v3** to convert qualified interest into the correct intake route.
4. Use **Trust v3** as the assurance and objection-handling companion.
5. Use **Student v3** after provider, price, age and activation gates are resolved.
6. Use **Resources v3** as an expertise asset after the referenced resources are published.
7. Use **Consumer v3** after Family and provider-entitlement validation.
8. Use **Legal v3** as a legal navigation/governance asset, not a primary sales deck.
9. Use **Prices v3** publicly only after pricing and commercial acceptance gates are approved.

## Unresolved gates

This ranking does not approve:

- proposed prices or MVA treatment;
- Family membership, age, guardian, privacy, entitlement or billing mechanics;
- provider delivery rights, licence/API basis, processor roles, availability or cost;
- usage measurement, limits, reset, upgrade or billing behaviour;
- legal, privacy, accessibility or contractual claims;
- operational intake staffing, status handling or response times;
- publication of planned resources or public links;
- public-production availability.
