## Applied design contract

- Canvas retained at 1920 × 1080.
- Primary margins and content width retained from the approved Atlas reference.
- Roboto Slab Regular used for primary headings.
- Roboto Slab SemiBold used for card headings.
- Roboto used for body, labels, notices and CTA text.
- Display H1: 72 px; dense H1: 54 px.
- Card titles: 27 px where dense content required it.
- Body and card body: 20–30 px according to hierarchy and available geometry.
- Labels: transparent fill, 2 px `#3257B8` border, 4 px radius.
- Cards: white fill, 1 px `#CFD2D8` border, 6 px radius, editable blue side accent where appropriate.
- CTA text: 24 px default, with fixed 78 px button height and source-appropriate fixed widths.
- Palette: `#3257B8`, `#15213F`, `#E9ECF2`, `#CFD2D8`, `#FFFFFF`.
