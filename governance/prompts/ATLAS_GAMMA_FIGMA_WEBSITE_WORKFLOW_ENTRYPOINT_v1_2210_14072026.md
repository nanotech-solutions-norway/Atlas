- v1 and v2 may differ in information architecture, not facts or status.
- Gamma is design/content evidence only.
- No unsupported operational, provider, security, legal, commercial or public-availability claims.
- No sensitive data in public forms.
- No secrets or confidential evidence in Gamma, Figma, GitHub, prompts, screenshots or reports.
- Business v1 measurements are a validated profile, not universal page geometry.
- Validate `SLIDE_GRID > SLIDE_ROW > SLIDE`, not only the immediate grid-helper result.
