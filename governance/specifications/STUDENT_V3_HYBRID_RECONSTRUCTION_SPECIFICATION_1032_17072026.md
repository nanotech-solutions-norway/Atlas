## Global design tokens

- Canvas: 1920 x 1080.
- Side margins: 104 px.
- Content width: 1712 px.
- Grid gap: 32 px.
- Card padding: 32 px.
- H1: Roboto Slab Regular, 54 px on content slides and 72 px on the hero.
- Body: Roboto Regular, 20-30 px.
- Subheadings: Roboto Slab SemiBold, 27-30 px, #3257B8 on white or light surfaces.
- Highlighted Gamma panel: #476FD6 with white text.
- Labels: transparent fill, 2 px #3257B8 stroke, 4 px radius, 44 px height, text width plus 28 px.
- Standard cards: white or #E9ECF2, 1 px #CFD2D8, 6 px radius.
- Manual-validation caution: #FFFBF5 / #B45309 / #15213F.
- Buttons: Roboto SemiBold **24 px**, 30 px line height, 78 px height, 2 px border, 4 px radius and 16 px gap.
