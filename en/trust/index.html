Trust Center

Privacy, control, transparency and claims discipline.

The Trust Center should pre-answer questions about data handling, providers, security, process, logging, incident readiness and limitations.

Form safety

Ordinary public forms are used only for low-risk qualification. Do not upload confidential or restricted files.

Provider disclosure

Atlas may use third-party AI engines. Data handling, retention, logging and training terms vary by provider and delivery mode.

AI output

AI outputs are support and drafts. Legal, financial, medical or compliance-critical decisions require human validation.

Claims registry

Public security, privacy, pricing and capability claims must have evidence_ref, owner, approval_status and review_date.
