## Norwegian copy standard

## Atlas ranking

Score each alternative from 0.0 to 10.0:

- Claim content and canonical alignment: 45%.
- Section structure and visual/source transferability: 20%.
- Readability and scanability: 15%.
- Sales, CTA and user value: 20%.

`overall = claim × 0.45 + structure × 0.20 + readability × 0.15 + sales × 0.20`

Interpretation:

- 9.0–10.0: strong controlled implementation candidate;
- 8.0–8.99: good draft with limited corrections;
- 7.0–7.99: significant correction required;
- below 7.0: unsuitable implementation source.

An alternative hard-fails regardless of score if it contradicts approved facts, implies open purchase/production access, presents Family as available, presents Managed Secure as operational, requests sensitive intake, uses unsupported claims, uses KI as the normal term, omits a material qualification or copies competitor wording.
