## Executive conclusion

The strongest Atlas position remains: **AI configured for real work, with onboarding, governance and defined boundaries.**

## Competitor groups

### Native platforms

OpenAI, Anthropic, Microsoft and Google increasingly combine strong capabilities, administration and privacy controls. Atlas cannot win by claiming a better model. Atlas wins by configuring the workflow, selecting the provider, defining the boundary and supporting adoption.

### Horizontal work tools

Notion, Grammarly, Perplexity, Canva, Gamma, Jasper and similar products solve portions of the user job. Atlas should integrate or recommend where fit, not reproduce generic product features.

### Norwegian/Nordic consultancies

Large consultancies offer credibility, breadth and enterprise access. Boutique AI agencies offer local language and speed. Atlas should differentiate through tightly scoped packages, visible process, evidence discipline and faster implementation for SMB/professional buyers.

### Education and consumer tools

Specialist learning and consumer tools have stronger product maturity and low prices. Atlas must avoid generic “AI tutor” or “life assistant” claims and focus on bounded Norwegian workflows, source discipline and clear usage limits.

### Governed enterprise AI platforms

Enterprise search, knowledge and agent platforms validate premium demand, but Atlas should not claim parity. Managed Secure must remain a controlled delivery route, not an enterprise-platform replacement.

## Current vendor-price interpretation

Provider pricing and features are volatile. OpenAI currently lists ChatGPT Business at $25/user/month when billed monthly and notes 2+ users; provider pages also change model access and controls frequently [W12]. Anthropic, Microsoft and Google maintain separate seat, plan and API structures [W13-W15].

**Strategic implication:** Atlas must not anchor consumer/student economics to retail seat resale. The service model requires a validated API/runtime or lawful provisioning path with hard cost controls.

## Positioning statement

**Atlas AI configures practical AI workflows for Norwegian businesses, students and households. We combine provider selection, onboarding, governance and defined usage boundaries so customers can move from scattered experimentation to repeatable use.**

Avoid:

- unsupported accuracy or grade claims;
- blanket GDPR compliance;

- market-share or customer-satisfaction percentages without Atlas evidence.
