**Is premium-model use unlimited?**  
No. Higher-cost capability use is controlled through defined monthly units and hard caps.

**What happens when the allowance is used?**  
Use may pause, be reduced or require a clearly approved plan change until the next reset. Atlas does not add chargeable usage without confirmation.

Not through ordinary public forms. Sensitive business material requires qualification and an approved controlled route.
