- Business: `Be om løsningsgjennomgang` / `Request solution review`.
- Operations/qualification: `Bestill kvalifiseringsmøte` only where the meeting route and expectation are approved.
- Managed Secure: `Be om arkitekturgjennomgang` / `Request architecture review`.
- Student/consumer: informational exploration or interest/contact only while purchase is disabled.
- Pricing: status and qualification, not purchase.

## 5. Copy controls

- One primary proposition and one primary CTA per section.
- Show actual use cases, process, exclusions and next steps.
- Do not use generic AI-futurist language, robots, glowing brains or unexplained neural imagery as proof.
- Do not claim guaranteed outcomes, certification, partnership, data location, retention or security beyond evidence.
- Every pricing or availability statement must show the current public/purchase status.
- Trust, provider, privacy and human-review boundaries must be visible before form completion.
