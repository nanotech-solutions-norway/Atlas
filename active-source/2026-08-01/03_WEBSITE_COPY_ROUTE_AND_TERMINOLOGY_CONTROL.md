## 6. Commercial and safety controls

- Approved public prices must remain visible.
- Public price does not mean purchase, payment, provisioning or activation is open.
- Student Basic: `129 kr/mnd. inkl. MVA`.
- Student Plus: `169 kr/mnd. inkl. MVA`.
- Everyday Basic: `159 kr/mnd. inkl. MVA`.
- Family: `399 kr/mnd. inkl. MVA`, status `Utsatt`; it cannot be purchased, reserved or activated.
- Managed Secure is qualification-only, not self-service and not guaranteed available.
- Ordinary public forms collect low-risk categorical information only and contain no file-upload field.
- Users must be warned not to submit confidential, sensitive or classified information.
- Student use begins at age 16; users aged 16–17 require an approved adult payer/contracting arrangement before activation.
- Provider use does not imply Atlas partnership, certification or ownership of provider controls.
- AI-generated output may be wrong, incomplete or outdated; human review remains required for consequential decisions.

## 9. Hard-fail controls

A hard fail overrides any numerical score. Hard fails include:

- incorrect approved price or MVA treatment;
- purchase or activation presented as open;
- Family presented as active;
- Managed Secure presented as operational, guaranteed or self-service;
- a public file-upload request;
- unsupported security, certification or provider claims;
- unqualified partnership assertions;
- normal use of KI outside the permitted Home explanation.
