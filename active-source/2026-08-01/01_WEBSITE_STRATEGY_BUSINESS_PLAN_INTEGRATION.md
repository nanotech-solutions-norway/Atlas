## 1. Strategic website role

The Phase 1 website is a controlled Norway-first public and commercial information surface. Its primary purpose is to:

1. explain Atlas AI and its governed delivery model;
2. make Business the dominant commercial entry point;
3. establish trust through accurate provider, data, human-review and availability boundaries;
4. generate qualified low-risk inquiries;
5. route higher-control needs to qualification and architecture review;
6. create a measurable foundation for later portal, payment, entitlement and secure-delivery phases.

Phase 1 is not a self-service subscription storefront and must not imply that paid plans, production entitlements or Managed Secure delivery are currently available.

## 2. Approved positioning

Atlas AI is a **Norway-first governed AI solution and integration layer** for organizations and selected target groups that need practical workflows, clear boundaries and controlled adoption of AI in real work.

The website must not position Atlas as:

- a proprietary foundation-model company;
- a generic prompt marketplace;
- a retail provider-seat reseller;
- an unrestricted AI consultancy without defined scope and controls;
- a fully implemented secure platform without evidence;
- certified, partnered or guaranteed where Atlas-specific evidence is absent.

## 3. Audience and conversion hierarchy

| Lane | Website priority | Phase 1 conversion objective | Current boundary |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Business/professional | Primary | Request solution review / qualification meeting | Low-risk inquiry and scoped human follow-up |

**Discovery -> understanding -> trust -> qualification -> human follow-up.**

## 5. Website content priorities

### Business

- Explain concrete workflow outcomes, not generic AI capability.
- Show problem -> governed workflow -> human review -> measurable next step.
- Use `Request solution review` as the primary CTA unless a page-specific approved CTA applies.
- Compress proof through validated examples, process evidence and bounded claims.

### Trust and Legal

Trust and Legal are first-class commercial assets, not footer-only compliance pages. They must distinguish:

- controls implemented now;
- provider- or configuration-dependent controls;
- target-state controls;
- unavailable or blocked capabilities;
- claims requiring approval or evidence.

### Contact and Onboarding

Contact, Onboarding and About remain separate roles:

- Contact: general low-risk contact and routing;
- Onboarding: controlled qualification and next-step process;
- About: entity, ownership, purpose and credibility.

No ordinary public form may collect confidential, restricted, special-category or highly sensitive information or files.

## 6. Platform strategy

- **Wix Studio:** retained as Phase 1 public CMS, multilingual, form, SEO and presentation surface.
- **Velo/custom code:** minimized; used only where justified by a documented requirement and validated security boundary.
- **GitHub:** canonical governance, routes, claims, schemas, QA and release evidence.
- **Google Drive:** controlled source archive, human-readable mirrors and private report formats.
- **Figma/Gamma:** design and content evidence only.
- **Azure or another protected runtime:** future candidate for authenticated, protected or confidential workflows; selection remains `PENDING_REVIEW`.

No CMS migration is approved. Webflow, WordPress and Vercel remain benchmark or future-option evidence only.

## 7. Accessibility, performance and language

- WCAG 2.2 AA is the internal design and QA target.
- The applicable Norwegian legal minimum remains separately recorded and must not be overstated.
- Manual testing is mandatory for keyboard, focus, contrast, forms, language attributes, reflow, responsive behavior and CMS/multilingual content.
- Core Web Vitals working targets for important pages are p75 LCP <= 2.5 s, INP <= 200 ms and CLS <= 0.1.
- Bokmål remains the root experience; English remains the reciprocal `/en/` mirror.
- English pages are published only when content, metadata, links and hreflang parity are complete.

## 8. SEO, AEO and GEO operating position

Atlas will prioritize fewer, stronger pages over thin content volume. Every publishable page requires:

- unique title, description, H1, canonical and index state;
- visible, crawlable principal content;
- clear direct-answer and next-step sections where useful;
- evidence-controlled claims;
- internal links between solution, pricing, trust and resources;
- reciprocal localization controls.

FAQ exists primarily to help users and provide clear answer content. FAQ structured data is used only where visible content and eligibility rules support it; rich-result eligibility is not a success claim.

## 9. Analytics and commercial learning

A consent-aware event architecture is approved for Phase 1. Minimum event groups:

- page and locale use;
- business, pricing, trust and Managed Secure CTA engagement;
- contact and onboarding form start/completion/error;
- FAQ search/expansion;
- resource engagement;
- trust/legal/pricing disclosure views;
- technical, accessibility and performance monitoring.

The analytics vendor is not selected by this update. Plausible and Matomo are preferred evaluation candidates; GA4 requires a separate purpose, consent and tagging decision.

After publication, at least three content/CTA hypotheses should be tested without bypassing claim, legal, privacy, accessibility or release controls.
