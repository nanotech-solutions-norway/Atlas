## Data and form controls

- Public forms use low-risk categorical information only.
- No public file upload.
- Do not request or accept confidential, sensitive or classified information in ordinary public forms.
- Form success text must not represent submission as an order or confirmed delivery.
- Contact and onboarding remain separate functions.

## Student controls

- Minimum intended age: 16.
- Users aged 16–17 require an approved adult payer or contracting arrangement before activation.
- Atlas may support planning, understanding and practice but not prohibited assessment substitution, fabricated sources or evasion of institution rules.

## Managed Secure controls

- Qualification-only.
- Not a self-service standard service.
- Not guaranteed available.
- No absolute security or error-free claim.
- Concrete architecture, data, identity, access, logging, human-control and operating responsibilities must be assessed.
- Qualification may result in refusal or a different delivery lane.

## Provider and claim controls

- Atlas delivers services around third-party technology.
- Provider platform use does not imply partnership.
- Provider certifications apply only to their documented scope and do not automatically transfer to Atlas or a customer solution.
- Every material claim requires source, scope, date, owner and review trigger.
- AI-generated responses may be wrong, incomplete or outdated.

## Hard-fail rules

A numerical score cannot override a hard fail. Hard fails include:

- incorrect price;
- purchase or activation presented as open;
- Family presented as active;
- Managed Secure presented as operational, guaranteed or self-service;
- public upload request;
- unsupported certification/security claim;
- unqualified provider or partnership assertion;
- routine use of KI outside the single Home explanation.
